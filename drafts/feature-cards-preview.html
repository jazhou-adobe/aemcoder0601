Get a rebate on your registration

You can apply to get a 20% rebate on your personal vehicle registration from FY 2025-2026.

You can claim this rebate for up to 2 light vehicles that are registered in your name.

Over 4 million eligible vehicles

Personal vehicles under 4.5 tonnes like cars, wagons, motorcycles, utes and light vans are all eligible for this rebate.

If your work vehicle is in your name, it's eligible for a rebate, but vehicles registered to organisations aren't eligible.

Apply with Service Victoria

To apply, you'll need to log in or create an account with Service Victoria and complete your application on the website or using the app. We'll let you know by email if your rebate has been approved.
